## Proposed research leadership

**MADAALA - proposed Principal Investigator**
A Human-Grounded Benchmark for Language Parity in Amharic and Afaan Oromo

MADAALA is an AAU-led research proposal examining whether AI systems provide comparably accurate, actionable, contextually appropriate and safe assistance in English, Amharic and Afaan Oromo. Planned work includes about 500 locally authored matched scenarios, native-speaker evaluation and independent calibration of automated judging.

Proposed responsibilities:

- Lead technical infrastructure for reproducible model evaluation.
- Mentor student contributors in implementation, testing and data quality assurance.
- Coordinate in-country project activities.

The repository provides a Python prototype with Bedrock and mock providers, dataset validation, controlled command-line execution, blinded response exports and evaluation utilities. A technical pilot generated 36 successful API responses; it does not establish validated model quality or language parity. The expanded benchmark, human evaluation and judge-calibration study are planned work.

Project: [MADAALA](https://github.com/wondimagegn/MADAALA)
Methodology: [Research design and analysis](https://github.com/wondimagegn/MADAALA/blob/main/docs/methodology.md)
